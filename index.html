I take ML from research idea to production system.

I'm an MS Computer Science student at NYU Courant (AI concentration), graduating in May 2027, with a background in electrical engineering from IIT Madras. I get excited by hard technical problems. My work balances ML engineering, systems and research, and my curiosity has also taken me into quantitative finance.

At Adobe, I developed a new video generation method and deployed a production inference API for Firefly. In research, I've pre-trained a genomic foundation model, cut communication costs in distributed and federated learning, and built sparse attention transformers for causal inference. At NYU, I'm working on efficient RL post-training that tackles reward hacking, and on reproducible GPU training.

I've also worked in quantitative finance at Morgan Stanley, building credit and bond risk analysis that went into production, and on anomaly detection for satellite telemetry at ISRO.

I love learning new things and understanding how they work all the way down, from the math to the GPU kernel. What I want most is to build something cutting-edge at real scale and see it running in production.
